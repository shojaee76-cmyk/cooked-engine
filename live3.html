what we refuse to do

No breached data, no leaked databases, no email or phone lookups, no login walls, no scraping behind an account, nothing about your family, nothing about your body. Public pages and public chains only. A roast you can screenshot, not a dossier for someone else.

COOKED reads public data only: chain state through public RPCs and keyless explorers, a public X profile endpoint, public ENS records and public profile pages. Wallets and handles you enter are cached for 15 minutes so the printer does not re-read the same chain twice, then dropped. No accounts, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no analytics. Gas in dollars is converted at today's gas price and marked as an estimate; the gas quantity itself is measured. The score is arithmetic you can audit in the readings panel.
